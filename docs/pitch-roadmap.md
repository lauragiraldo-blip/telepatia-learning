# Demo run-of-show: 1 minute 50 seconds

Goal: show the entire loop (student writes → engine audits → gold standard →
faculty data) and prove it's real, inside 110 seconds.

## Before you're called up (not counted)

1. Open the demo full screen ("abrirlo a pantalla completa" on patonews).
2. Pre-stage Caso 04: create it, dictate two sentences, generate the AI note,
   and open "Comparar con el estándar de oro" once so the AI comparison is
   generated and cached. Then click back to Caso 03. Both cases sit warm.
3. Leave the screen on the Caso 03 case brief (scene 1 dot).
4. AUTO off. You navigate by clicking the dots in the pill (1 click per jump).

## The 110 seconds

| Clock | Screen (dot) | You say (deliver in your own words) |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:12 | Case brief | "Telepatía already writes the notes and audits the doctors in our client hospitals. This is that same engine, pointed at medical students. A student picks a case and works it like a real consultation: by voice, or uploading their handwritten record." |
| 0:12–0:25 | Student note (dot 4) | "The student worked the case and wrote this record. And like every real student, they made mistakes. Watch what happens when they submit." |

| 0:55–1:10 | Gold standard (dot 6) | "Then the note goes side by side against the gold standard approved by faculty. Red is the student's deviation, green is the standard. This is how you learn to write a record." |

## Rules that keep you inside 1:50

- **Never type or dictate live.** Everything risky is pre-staged; the live
  proof is showing the pre-generated Caso 04 note (1 click).
- **One sentence per click.** Click the dot, breathe, say the line. The
  animations (gauge, bars, findings) buy you the pause.
- **The feedback screen is the pitch.** If you're running long, cut the gold
  standard beat, never cut feedback or the close.
- **Rehearse 3 times with a phone timer.** Target 1:40 in rehearsal; adrenaline
  eats 10 seconds.

## If something breaks

- Wifi/server down → local copy: double-click `prototype/index.html`.
- Everything down → play `demo.mp4` (autoplay run, ~1:26) and narrate over it.
- Projector dies → the narrative table above works with zero screen: tell the
  loop, end with the slogan.
