The real return

The fee is the hook.
The habit is the business.

Priced low on purpose. A student who learns medicine with Telepatía never learns to practice without it — so the semester fee isn’t the return. The graduate is.

Cheap now → daily habit → B2B pull + B2C for a career.

Anchors: programs already pay $100–250/student/year for question banks; simulation centers cost $300K–1M in capex.
